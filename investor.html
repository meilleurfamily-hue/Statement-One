Investor guide

How to explore the Operational Preview

1. Enter the preview. Select the gold button below to open the demonstration dashboard.
2. Start with Dashboard and Occurrences. Explore the navigation and open a fictional occurrence to understand the intended case workflow.
3. Review officer tools. Look for officer notes, witness statements, evidence packages and related occurrence actions. Test functionality varies across modules.
4. Explore Supervisor View. Use the supervisor navigation to see the intended review and correction process. The workflow is not yet fully validated.
5. Return to this introduction. Use your browser Back button or revisit /investor.html.
