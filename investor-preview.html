Investor User Guide

Use fictional sample information only. This is a prototype and some features may be incomplete.

  1. Choose Explore Operational Preview above to open the application.
  2. On the dashboard, explore an existing sample occurrence or create a fictional one.
  3. Open the record and review the available notes, witness and evidence sections.
  4. Review the displayed history, timestamps and supervisor review options.
  5. If Settings offers a role switch, choose Supervisor to explore the review queue.
  6. Return here using your browser Back button. Avoid entering real case data.

Important demonstration limitations

This is a prototype for discussion and evaluation, not an approved law-enforcement system. Some features and integrations are incomplete or simulated. Do not enter real personal information, operational police data, or evidence. Security, persistence, access controls, audit integrity and full workflow reliability require independent verification before deployment.
